## 📊 技術總結

- **Buffer Zone 分層：** 包裝食品 ±15%/12%/35%，生鮮 ±20%/20%/40%
- **Anchor Priority：** Geo Cluster → CC Median → Trimmed Mean → AFCD Wholesale
- **跨家庭門檻：** trust_score ≥ 0.3，min 5 households / 10 data points
- **Geo Clustering：** 1.5km Haversine distance
- **Community Badge：** 前 5% 用戶獎勵，省錢 vs district avg
